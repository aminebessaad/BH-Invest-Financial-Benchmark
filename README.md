An exhaustive, multi-firm quantitative financial benchmark and competitive intelligence study analyzing **16 active brokerage houses (intermédiaires en bourse)** in Tunisia, conducted using official regulatory filings from the **Conseil du Marché Financier (CMF)** and the **Bourse de Tunis (BVMT)** across the 2024–2025 financial years.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary & Key Findings

* **Exhaustive Market Coverage:** Covers 16 financial intermediaries operating on the BVMT (*Amen Invest, AFC, Attijari Intermédiation, BH INVEST, BNA Capitaux, BTK Conseil, CGI, MAC SA, MAXULA, MCP, SBT, STB Finance, Tunisie Valeurs, UIB Finance, Union Capital, and UFI*).
* **Capital & Solvency Resilience:** Evaluates balance sheet strength across the sector, highlighting **BH INVEST's robust capital base (10.553 MD in equity, 7th sector rank)** and total asset growth of **+45.43% to 26.875 MD**.
* **Public Banking Peer Outperformance:** Demonstrates consistent outperformance of BH INVEST against its primary public banking peer (**STB Finance**) across net income (**0.541 MD vs. 0.264 MD**), return on equity (**ROE: 5.13% vs. 1.67%**), and net profit margin (**19.84% vs. 9.75%**).
* **Collective Asset Management (OPCVM) Expansion:** Identifies asset management as the primary recurring growth engine; assets under management (AUM) surged by **+24.67% to 379 MD**, driving a **+26.24% increase in management fees (1.217 MD)**.
* **Cost Structure & Operational Productivity Levers:** Examines fixed-cost rigidities during cyclic equity market downturns, analyzing cost-to-income (*coefficient d'exploitation*) at **119.36%** and personnel cost ratios at **79.02%**, outlining key operational productivity levers.
* **Financial Portfolio Sensitivity:** Deconstructs the role of non-operating placement revenues (1.141 MD) and exceptional asset disposals (+0.600 MD capital gain on BH Assurance) in underwriting net profitability.

## 🛠️ Methodology

1. **Financial Modeling & Excel Engine:**
   * Automated cross-firm financial statement normalization across balance sheets, income statements, and off-balance sheet AUM metrics.
   * Multi-variable ratio computation: Return on Equity (ROE), Net Profit Margin, Cost-to-Income, Personnel Expense Ratio, Placement Income Weights, and Average Management Fee Rates.
2. **Executive Financial Analysis:**
   * Benchmarked capital allocation efficiency, solvency metrics, operational cost structures, and non-operating revenue sensitivity.
